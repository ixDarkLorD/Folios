Folios A guide for every mod

Two ways to read

On the screen, or in your hands

Every page works in both. A book picks its style, and players get the same content either way.

Screen books

A flat book in the middle of the screen with the world dimmed around it: ribbon tabs, bookmarks, search, and light or dark paper.

Handheld books

Raised from the hotbar and opened in your hands, its leaves turning in the world. Players nearby see you reading, and can read along.
