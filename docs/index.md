# Folios

**Guide books for Minecraft mods, written as JSON in a resource pack.** Give your mod a manual players actually want to
open: a book on the screen, or held open in their hands in the world, with recipes, builds, quests and more.

[Get started](docs/getting-started.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary }
[CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/folios){ .md-button }
[Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/folios){ .md-button }

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-book-open-page-variant:{ .lg .middle } **Two ways to read**

    ---

    A flat book in the middle of the screen with tabs, bookmarks and search, or a book raised from the hotbar and
    opened in your hands, its leaves turning in the world. Players nearby see you reading and can read along.

-   :material-file-document-multiple:{ .lg .middle } **Every kind of page**

    ---

    Text with a small Markdown, spotlights, images, recipes, turning 3D scenes of entities and builds, maps of
    linked places, quests, and page types of your own.

-   :material-cube-outline:{ .lg .middle } **Builds you can follow**

    ---

    A build page lists the blocks it needs and stands the build in the world as ghost blocks, layer by layer, with a
    panel tracking what is left.

-   :material-trophy:{ .lg .middle } **Quests and progress**

    ---

    Objectives followed live, toasts when a quest is done, celebrations when a category or the whole book is
    mastered, and entries locked behind advancements or flags.

-   :material-palette:{ .lg .middle } **Yours to restyle**

    ---

    Books are plain files in a resource pack: packs can add, translate and restyle them, and `F3 + T` reloads them
    while you write.

-   :material-code-braces:{ .lg .middle } **Built for mods**

    ---

    Open books from code, give players book items, and add your own page types, reveal effects and objectives.

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: For mod developers](docs/for-mod-developers.md)

</div>

## Versions

| Minecraft | Loaders |
|---|---|
| 26.1 – 26.3 | Fabric, NeoForge |
| 1.21 – 1.21.1 | Fabric, NeoForge, Forge |
| 1.20.1 | Fabric, Forge |

Folios needs [CoolCatLib](https://modrinth.com/mod/coolcatlib), and Fabric API on Fabric.
